FAQ — 06

Common
Questions

Do I need prior experience? +

No prior experience required for most tracks. Our introductory courses are designed to take you from complete beginner to professional. For advanced masterclasses, some familiarity with the basics is helpful but not mandatory.

What equipment do I need? +

For video editing and graphic design, a basic laptop with 8GB RAM is sufficient to start. For photography and cinematography, we provide professional equipment during the course. The SERI Space also has workstations available for graduates who need them.

How long are the courses? +

Our intensive tracks run 4-8 weeks with evening and weekend sessions. Comprehensive programs run 12 weeks. All classes are designed to accommodate working professionals.

Do you provide certification? +

Yes. Upon completing your capstone project, you receive a Seri Certified Creative accreditation recognized by our partner agencies. However, we prioritize your portfolio over paper — your work samples matter more than the certificate.

Can I work while studying? +

Absolutely. Our courses are designed for working professionals. Evening and weekend sessions mean you can keep your current job while building your creative skills. Many students start taking small freelance gigs before graduating.

What career support do you offer after graduation? +

SERI graduates get lifetime access to our alumni network, client referrals, job board, and portfolio reviews. We also host monthly meetups and workshops to keep your skills and network growing. Our goal is not just to teach you, but to launch your career.
