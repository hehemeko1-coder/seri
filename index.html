GOOD QUESTIONS — 06

Before you
begin.

It’s normal to have questions before trying something new.

Do I need prior experience? +

Most introductory tracks are designed for beginners. You can start with curiosity and build your skills step by step.

What equipment do I need? +

For editing and design, a basic laptop is a useful starting point. For camera-based tracks, ask the admissions team about equipment access for your course.

How long are the courses? +

Course length and schedules can vary by track. Request the course guide for the latest program details.

Can I study while working? +

Ask about current evening and weekend options so you can find a schedule that works with your commitments.

Will I build a portfolio? +

The learning approach emphasizes practical projects so you can develop work samples as you build your skills.
